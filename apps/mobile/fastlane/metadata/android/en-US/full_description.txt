Continuum Calendar is a private, open-source schedule planner for Android — forked from Fossify Calendar.

What you get:

AD-FREE AND PRIVATE:
Your events stay on your device and accounts you choose. No ads, no tracking SDKs.

FLEXIBLE EVENTS:
Times, durations, reminders, and advanced repetition rules.

CALDAV SYNC:
Connect CalDAV providers (including Google Calendar over CalDAV) when you want cloud calendars.

WIDGETS & VIEWS:
Month, day, week, year, and agenda (event list) views plus home-screen widgets.

OPEN SOURCE:
This Android app is GPL-3.0-only (Fossify Calendar fork). Desktop and other Continuum trees stay MIT — see LICENSING.md in the repository.

Download Continuum Calendar and keep your schedule on your terms.
