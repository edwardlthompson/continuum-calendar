Continuum Calendar is a private, open-source schedule planner for Android — forked from Fossify Calendar with Continuum branding and desktop peer sync.

What you get:

AD-FREE AND PRIVATE:
Your events stay on your device and accounts you choose. No ads, no tracking SDKs.

FLEXIBLE EVENTS:
Times, durations, reminders, and advanced repetition rules.

GOOGLE & CALDAV SYNC:
Connect Google Calendar (and other CalDAV providers) when you want cloud calendars.

CONTINUUM DESKTOP PEER SYNC:
Optional settings and local-event sync with Continuum Calendar on desktop via Google Drive App Data.

WIDGETS & VIEWS:
Month, day, week, year, and agenda (event list) views plus home-screen widgets.

OPEN SOURCE:
MIT-licensed Continuum product shell; the Android app remains GPL-3 as a Fossify Calendar fork — see LICENSE and credits in the repository.
